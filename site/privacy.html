Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-08-07

No browsing history leaves your machine. DeepWork Tab reads your open tabs and your typed reasons locally, using Chrome's storage.local, to run the tab cap and the queue. None of that — tab titles, URLs, or anything you type — is ever sent anywhere.

The only network request the extension makes is one anonymous daily ping to a Cloudflare Worker at deepwork-tab-ping.kumarbharath63.workers.dev. That ping contains a random install ID and the current day — nothing else. It exists so we know the extension is still in use; it cannot be tied back to you, your tabs, or your browsing.

Permissions the extension requests and why:

  • tabs — to count open tabs against your cap and to close or reopen queued tabs.
  • storage — to save your cap, queue, and reasons locally on your device.
  • alarms — to schedule the once-daily anonymous ping.
  • host permissions (all sites) — to detect which URL you were about to open when the intercept fires; no page content is read.

Uninstalling the extension deletes all locally stored data. The full source is public at github.com/Bharath-code/deepwork-tab if you'd rather verify than trust.
