Your editor has a focus mode. Your browser doesn't.

DeepWork Tab caps your open tabs. Hit the cap and a calm screen asks you to close one or queue it — nothing is ever lost.

  1. Set a tab cap — default is 7.
  2. Open one too many and a full-page intercept steps in, calm and clear.
  3. Close a tab or queue it for later — the queue lives in the toolbar popup, nothing gets lost.

No browsing history ever leaves your machine. The extension sends one anonymous daily ping (an install ID and the day) so we know it's still useful. Read the privacy policy.
